# Home image assets

Mode: built-in `image_gen` tool. Intent: generate new photographs from the supplied home-screen design reference. No network image loading is required at runtime.

Final files:

- `ride_hero.jpg` — 1440 × 810 px; alpine cycling hero background.
- `central_park.jpg` — 480 × 480 px; park docking station thumbnail.
- `riverside.jpg` — 480 × 480 px; riverside docking station thumbnail.

Images were inspected after generation. System.Drawing was used only for proportional downsampling and JPEG conversion (quality 88). No crop or creative edits were applied.

## ride_hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background photograph for a bicycle mobility app hero card. Create a wide landscape photograph, approximately 16:9, of a pristine turquoise alpine lake surrounded by rugged rocky green mountains with small patches of snow. A black mountain bike's large front wheel, fork, handlebars and a little frame dominate the RIGHT THIRD of the photograph, the wheel rests on natural rocks at the edge of the lake. The bike may extend beyond the right edge. LEFT TWO THIRDS are open background: dark deep teal lake and green mountain, suitable for separately overlaid white UI text, so keep the left side calm and quite dark. Natural daylight, realistic sharp bicycle details, rich fresh greens, editorial outdoor travel photography. No person, no text, no logo, no watermark, no border, no UI, no buttons. Deliver one wide image.

## central_park

Eye-level perspective looks along the parked bikes; bikes visible in foreground and trees fill the background. Sunny soft daylight filtered through leaves, natural realistic park photography, fresh green palette. Square composition, simple readable visual at thumbnail size. No people, no words, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no border.
